Measure it

Pick Region 1 or Region 2 in the toolbar, then click (or drag a box) on the image. The same spot is measured in both scans. Put Region 1 on bone and Region 2 on air.

Values are % of each scan's display range. CNR = |Region 1 − Region 2| ÷ noise (SD) in Region 2: higher means bone stands out more clearly against the grain.

Scans: NSI X500, Comet microfocal source, 72.28 µm voxels, 7200 views, 220 µA. Slices are registered to a common grid and cropped to the region all scans share. Simulated images: the 7200-projection image is the real scan. Images with fewer projections were simulated from it by re-projecting the slice (parallel-beam approximation) and rebuilding it from every 2nd, 4th, 8th… view, with random noise added to match the real scan’s noise level. Exact streak patterns will differ somewhat from a true re-reconstruction from the raw projections.
